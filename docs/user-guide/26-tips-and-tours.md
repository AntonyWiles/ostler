# Tips and tours

Ostler can point things out as you go: small notes that sit beside the part of the window they
explain, which glows softly for a moment so you can see what the note is about. Where there is
something to click, that control gets a ring of its own, with a word or two such as **Click to
sort**. Where there is something to drag, where it can go is outlined and a faint copy glides there
a few times, with a word such as **Drag to another day**. They never cover the window, never dim it
and never stop you working; click anywhere else and carry on. If your system is set to reduce
motion, nothing moves: the rings and outlines simply show. For now they start in the sample project,
"Welcome to Ostler (Demo Project)"
([The sample project](01-getting-started.md#the-sample-project)).

## The tour

The first time you open the sample project, a note under its name offers a short tour.

- **Show me around** starts it: fourteen short steps. After the project's name and its tabs, it
  visits each view in turn (Overview, Board, List, Calendar, Timeline, Milestones, Library and
  Decisions), says what that view is for and marks what to try there, then ends with **New task**,
  Search and Help. The tour opens each view for you.
- Move with **Next** and **Back**, or click a dot to jump to that step. With the tour focused, the
  `Left` and `Right` arrow keys move too. The last step's **Done** ends it.
- **X** or `Esc` closes the tour part way. It isn't offered again by itself.
- **Not now** (or the invitation's **X**) puts it away until Ostler next starts.
- **Don't show this again** turns the invitation down for good.

Take the tour again any time: **Help › Take the tour**, the command palette (`Ctrl` + `K`, then
"tour"), or **Take the tour** in Settings › General › Tips and tours. If the sample project was
removed, Ostler makes it again first.

## Tips

Once the tour is answered, the sample project has one tip for each of a few places, shown the first
time you are there and only one at a time: a task's details, the Board, the List, the Calendar,
the Timeline and the Library. A tip the tour already covered isn't shown again.

- **Got it** closes the tip for good.
- **X** puts it away until Ostler next starts.
- **Turn off tips** turns every tip off. A message offers **Turn back on** straight away.

While a whiteboard is open, neither the invitation nor a tip appears over it; they wait until you
leave the board.

## Turning tips off or on

Settings › General › Tips and tours:

- **Show tips** turns the tips and the tour's invitation off or on. With it off, you can still take
  the tour from Help.
- **Show dismissed tips again** brings back every tip you closed with **Got it**, and the tour's
  invitation.

## With a keyboard or screen reader

A tip doesn't take focus when it appears, so typing carries on where it was. A screen reader
announces its title, and each tip is a region named after its title, so it can be found with the
screen reader's list of regions or landmarks. When you start the tour, focus moves to the tour so
`Tab`, `Enter` and the arrow keys work it straight away.
